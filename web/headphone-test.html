GreenDot headphone test

Checks whether a tap on your headphones reaches this page, including after the microphone has been used.

This page must be opened over https:// or the microphone will not work.

Steps

  1. Connect your headphones. Turn the volume up and turn on Do Not Disturb.
  2. Tap Start. Keep this page open; don't lock the phone.
  3. Put the phone down. Press the headphone button 3 times, a few seconds apart. Each press should beep.
  4. Turn on Record after each press. Press again, say something for 3 seconds, and listen to it played back. Repeat 3 times.
  5. Tap Copy report and send it, or take a screenshot of the Results box.

Beep, record 3 s, beep, play it back, then wait for the next press.
